## Story

LocalBiz AI helps a small business turn its product information into a marketing draft,
a branded product-photo poster, and a practical posting plan in one workspace.

## Submission description

LocalBiz AI is an MVP marketing workspace for local businesses. Users save their business
profile and products, generate three editable social-post drafts with Gemini, create free
branded posters from their own product photos, and organize manual posting dates in a saved
calendar. The React/Vite frontend and Express/MongoDB backend provide account-private data,
sessions, poster downloads, and Sri Lanka posting times. Automatic social publishing is a
future feature.
